Projects

Android app · Coffee ordering

Brewffe

A coffee ordering app built around one idea: getting your daily cup in just a few taps. Customers sign in, put together a drink the way they like it, and look back at what they have ordered.

  • Sign-up and login that checks your input and clearly tells you when something worked or went wrong
  • A step-by-step order builder for choosing a drink, a size and add-ons, with the total updating as you pick
  • An orders page showing how many orders you made and how much you spent, with the option to remove an order
  • Android
  • Mobile UI

Mobile app · Pantry and budget tracker

SariTrack

An app that helps Filipino households keep track of the food in their kitchen and the money they spend on it. It shows what is about to expire, what to buy next and how the month's grocery budget is holding up.

  • A dashboard with expiry alerts, a shopping list and a pantry health score at a glance
  • Recipe ideas based on what is already in the pantry, powered by the Gemini API
  • Barcode scanning for adding items, plus a finance page with monthly charts, a category breakdown and a purchase log
  • Android (Java)
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • Gemini API
  • UI Design

UI/UX design · Interactive prototype

Cafe Rencontre

A concept for the ordering and point-of-sale screens of Cafe Rencontre, the café of LCUP-CITHM. The design aims for a warm, cozy feel that matches the coffee shop itself.

  • An order summary that works out tax, discount and change, and supports several payment methods
  • Guest, login and registration paths so anyone can start ordering right away

  • UI/UX

A mobile app that lets student organizations under LCUP CITE run their activities from one place. It covers planning events, keeping track of inventory and budgets, and putting together reports, with different tools for officers, faculty advisers and admins.
